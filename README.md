Steady Steel is a CWB‑certified steel fabrication company based in Newmarket, Ontario. It offers structural and miscellaneous steel, aluminum and stainless steel services including design, cutting, welding and installation across York Region and the Greater Toronto Area. The company has been operating since 2018 and serves both commercial and residential projects with a focus on quality and timely delivery.

**Areas:** Steel · Fabrication · Manufacturing · Newmarket · Ontario

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Steady Steel: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
